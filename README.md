"WALLS" is a simple VR game built with Unity for the Meta Quest 3. 

# Menu Screen
On the menu screen, you can either click 'Play' or 'Quit' to start or quit the game, respectively.

# Game State
Once playing the game, you will notice there are 4 walls surrounding you. They slowly close in on you from all directions.
The player must shoot balls (by pressing trigger) at the walls to push them back. The goal is to keep any of the walls from touching you for as long as possible.
If one of the walls touch you, the game goes back to the title screen.
You can turn your head 360 degrees to see the walls, and you may use either controller to throw balls. 
Balls are thrown from your eye. Holding down trigger for longer periods of time increases their velocity.
